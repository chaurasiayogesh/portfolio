06 · Creative Direction & Quality Governance

Creative Direction: What Truly Matters & My 6 Approval Questions

High-Converting Outdoor, Print & Digital Standards
EXECUTIVE CREATIVE PHILOSOPHY

In real estate marketing, a creative is never mere decoration — it is a high-stakes financial instrument responsible for driving site gallery walk-ins and converting multi-crore inventory. Every billboard, newspaper jacket, and digital reel must pass strict visual ergonomics, psychological hooks, and zero-error legal governance before receiving my sign-off.

Part 1: The 4 Non-Negotiables When Designing Real Estate Creatives

PILLAR 01

The 3-Second Highway Rule

Commuters driving at 60 km/h on expressways or scrolling through feeds give an ad 3 seconds flat. Headlines must not exceed 6–7 words with massive contrast legibility from 100+ meters. Zero tiny paragraphs.

Metric: Instant Comprehension Speed
PILLAR 02

Hyper-Local Value Hook

Avoid generic cliches ("Luxury Living"). High-intent buyers look for hard numbers: Configuration + Starting Ticket Size + Infrastructure Hook (e.g. "2 BHK Deck Homes at ₹65L · 2 Mins to Metro Line 5").

Metric: Qualified Inbound Intent
PILLAR 03

Emotion + Rational Urgency

Real estate is an emotional decision justified rationally. Connect homebuyer family pride ("Naye Ghar Ki Shuruat") with logical urgency: Mivan speed milestones, possession dates, or festive price benefits.

Metric: Weekend Footfall Surge
PILLAR 04

Channel-Native Craftsmanship

Formats demand custom execution: Hoardings need night halogen contrast; Newspapers need dot-gain ink compensation; Meta Reels need 9:16 thumb-stoppers; and RERA must be crystal-clear.

Metric: Production & Regulatory Integrity
